Products

BLV Enterprises LLC develops and publishes mobile applications. Our first product is Over Their Lives.

Over Their Lives

Over Their Lives is a mobile app that helps a parent pray over their children intentionally, consistently and holistically. It is Christian and scripture-based.

The app is for the parent, not the child. A parent uses it to pray for each of their children; children do not have accounts.

Three dimensions of prayer

Prayers in Over Their Lives are organised across three dimensions of a child's life:

Body
Health, protection, sleep and growth.
Soul
Mind, emotions, friendships and courage.
Spirit
Faith, identity and purpose.

Chosen for a child's age

Prayers are chosen for the age of the child being prayed for, across three age bands:

  • 0–5 years
  • 6–12 years
  • 13–18 years
