BLV Enterprises LLC

BLV Enterprises LLC is a software and media company based in Branchburg, New Jersey. We develop mobile applications and publish faith and family content on our own social media channels.

The company is a New Jersey limited liability company, formed in 2026. This website is the company's own site: it sets out what BLV Enterprises LLC does, the products it publishes, and how to reach it.

We design, build and publish mobile applications for iPhone and Android. Our first application is Over Their Lives, a prayer app for parents.

Our first product

Over Their Lives

Over Their Lives is a mobile app that helps a parent pray over their children intentionally, consistently and holistically. It is Christian and scripture-based, and is made for the parent rather than the child.

Over Their Lives is developed and published by BLV Enterprises LLC for iPhone and Android, and launches on Christmas Day, December 25, 2026.
